Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How long does it take to armour a vehicle?

Typical lead times are 6–8 weeks for CIT vans and simpler platforms; 8–12 weeks for SUVs and sedans; and 14–16+ weeks for custom tactical platforms. Lead time starts from confirmed order and vehicle handover to our Delhi facility.

Can you armour a vehicle I already own?

Yes — we accept client-supplied vehicles for armoring following a pre-inspection for structural integrity and condition. Most platforms under 60,000 km and 5 years old are accepted. We will advise during the consultation call.

Are your blast protection products certified?

Yes. Our Bomb Suppression Blankets (BSB) are approved by the Government of India's Ministry of Defence, tested by the Terminal Ballistic Research Laboratory (TBRL) under DRDO. Our Blast Containment Bins are certified to MHA 2013 standards, rated for 2 KG TNT blast equivalence.

Do you supply to government and defence organisations?

Yes — government, defence, and paramilitary procurement is our core business. We have supplied the Indian Air Force, Navy, ISRO, CRPF, BSF, CISF, and state police forces. We support DGS&D, GeM portal, and direct tender procurement processes.

What protection levels do you manufacture to?

We manufacture armoured vehicles from B4 (handgun-level) to B7+ (armour-piercing rifle rounds) per CEN EN 1063, and up to VR10 (explosive resistant) per the ERV 2022 standard. The appropriate level is selected based on a threat environment assessment during consultation.

Where is Secure Mobile India located?

Our factory and head office are at A-20 Adhyapak Nagar, Najafgarh Road, Delhi — 110 041. We have been manufacturing at this location since our founding in 1995. All products are designed and fabricated here in India.
