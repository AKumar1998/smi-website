Our BR Range

Complete Bullet
Resistant Solutions

Secure Mobile India manufactures a comprehensive range of Bullet Resistant (BR) products that protect personnel, assets and spaces against ballistic threats. Every product is designed to NIJ, CEN EN and BIS standards, and tested at DRDO's Terminal Ballistic Research Laboratory (TBRL) — the highest ballistic testing authority in India.

Our BR products serve the Indian Army, Police forces, CRPF, NSG, VIP protection agencies, banks, government offices, embassies and private organisations across India and internationally.

Our BR Products

Bullet Resistant Vest

Soft and hard body armour in concealable and overt configurations. UHMWPE and aramid construction for superior comfort and all-day wearability across NIJ IIIA through IV protection levels.

View Product
Bullet Resistant Helmet

Ballistic helmets in PASGT, MICH and high-cut ACH profiles. Aramid and UHMWPE composite shells rated for fragmentation and handgun threats with optional night-vision and communication mounts.

View Product
Bullet Resistant Shield

Transparent and opaque BR Shields for close-protection, law enforcement entries and convoy operations. NIJ Level III and IIIA variants with ergonomic carry handles and arm straps.

View Product
BR Sentry Cabin

Prefabricated bullet resistant sentry cabins for checkpoint and perimeter security. B4–B6 rated steel construction with A/C provision, CCTV mounts, intercom ports, and rapid on-site assembly.

View Product
BR Lecture Stand

Ballistic lecture stands for VIP addresses, ministerial briefings and state functions. Multi-layer BR glass front panel rated to B5/B6 in a discreet design indistinguishable from a standard podium.

View Product
BR Patka

A bullet resistant Patka designed specifically for Sikh military and police personnel who wear a Patka under their operational helmet. Multi-layer aramid fabric construction provides fragmentation and handgun ballistic protection without compromising religious observance or comfort during prolonged field operations.

View Product
BR Morcha

A portable and static bullet resistant Morcha — a covered firing and observation position for infantry and paramilitary units. Provides B5/B6 ballistic cover while maintaining an unobstructed field of fire. Deployed across border security posts, anti-insurgency operations and static checkpoints.

View Product
BR Observation Tower

Prefabricated bullet resistant observation towers for border surveillance, perimeter watch and forward operating bases. Ballistic-rated glazing for 360° visibility and steel structure rated to B5/B6 against rifle threats, with provision for communication and CCTV systems.
