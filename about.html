Our Story

India's Most
Trusted Defence
Partner

Established in 1995 and led by Mr. Shiv Yadav, Secure Mobile India began with a clear mandate: to build world-class ballistic protection products that meet the unique security demands of the Indian subcontinent — designed and made entirely in India.

From our facility in Nangloi, Delhi, we have spent three decades manufacturing armoured vehicles, bomb suppression blankets, blast containment systems, and defensive equipment trusted by India's most sensitive organisations — including the Indian Air Force, the Indian Navy, ISRO, CRPF, BSF, CISF, and state police forces across the country.

Every product we build is tested to the highest standards — ballistic approval from DRDO's Terminal Ballistic Research Laboratory, and compliance with Ministry of Home Affairs guidelines. We are proud to have protected invaluable lives for over 30 years.

Our Principles

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To engineer, manufacture and deliver the highest quality ballistic protection products and armoured mobility solutions — designed and made in India — that empower India's defence, law enforcement, and government institutions to operate safely in any threat environment.

Our Vision

To be Asia's most trusted, innovation-led ballistic protection manufacturer — setting new benchmarks in bomb suppression technology, armoured vehicle engineering, and indigenous defence fabrication that keeps India's people and institutions safe from evolving threats.

Our Values

Integrity in every build. Zero compromise on quality. Deep respect for the personnel whose lives depend on our products. Innovation ahead of the threat curve. And an unwavering commitment to Aatmanirbhar Bharat — protecting India with India's own ingenuity.

Our Journey

Three Decades
of Protection

1995

Company Founded

Secure Mobile India established in Delhi by Mr. Shiv Yadav with a focused mandate to manufacture indigenous ballistic protection products for India's security forces.

Late 1990s

First Government Supply

Early bulletproof vehicle conversions and sentry posts delivered to state police and government security establishments, establishing SMI's reputation for quality and reliability.

2000s

Bomb & Blast Product Range Launched

SMI invents and introduces India's first indigenous Bomb Suppression Blanket (BSB) with Blast Containment Ring (BCR) — later approved by DRDO's Terminal Ballistic Research Laboratory.

2005–2010

Blast Resistant Tactical Bunker

Invention and deployment of India's first domestic Blast Resistant Tactical Bunker, adopted by the Indian Air Force, Navy, ISRO, and police forces across the country.

2013

MHA Standard Compliance

Our Blast Containment Bin is tested and certified to the Ministry of Home Affairs 2013 standard — rated for 2 KG TNT blast protection, enabling deployment at India's major public infrastructure.

2025

30+ Years & Counting

Over three decades, thousands of products delivered, and India's most sensitive institutions protected. SMI continues to innovate, now focusing on anti-drone and next-generation blast mitigation systems.
