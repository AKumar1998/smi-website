Product Overview

Advance Under
Fire

SMI Bullet Resistant Shields are designed to provide dependable ballistic protection during tactical deployment, high-risk law enforcement operations, convoy movement, and hostile engagement scenarios.

Manufactured using advanced ballistic-grade materials, the shields are engineered to provide effective resistance against small arms fire while maintaining manageable operational weight and handling comfort.

The ergonomic grip and arm support system allows stable shield handling during prolonged deployment while enabling operational mobility in dynamic tactical environments.
