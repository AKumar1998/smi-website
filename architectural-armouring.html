Our Approach

From Site Survey
to Certified Handover

SMI's architectural armouring process begins with a threat assessment and site survey by our technical team. We model ballistic and blast exposures for each elevation, specify the appropriate protection levels for glazing, walls and structure, and supply turnkey installation through our certified contractor network.

All architectural armouring is independently tested and certified. We work closely with project architects and MEP consultants to ensure that protection is integrated without compromising building aesthetics, natural light or mechanical performance.

Standards Reference

EN 1063
Ballistic Standards

EN 1063 defines internationally recognized ballistic protection standards for bullet-resistant glazing systems, ranging from handgun threats to armor-piercing rifle ammunition. SMI Architectural Armouring solutions are engineered according to operational threat assessments and certified ballistic performance requirements.

Our ballistic glazing systems undergo rigorous evaluation and testing procedures to ensure dependable protection performance for embassies, command centers, secure infrastructure, financial institutions, and high-risk architectural environments.

Structural Protection

Blast-Resistant Doors,
Walls & Cladding

Ballistic and blast hardening extends beyond glazing. SMI supplies and installs the full building envelope — armoured doors and frames, ballistic wall cladding panels and blast-resistant partition systems — all to certified protection levels.

Blast-Resistant Doors & Frames

Steel-core armoured door leaf with blast-rated perimeter frame and multi-point locking. Rated for blast overpressure from 50 mbar to 1,000 mbar per ISO 16933.

  • Door thickness: 50–120 mm composite
  • Blast rating: 50–1,000 mbar overpressure
  • Ballistic core: RHA / UHMWPE composite
  • Frame: Welded steel with rebate seal
  • Optional: vision panel up to BR6
  • Electromechanical or manual locking

Ballistic Wall Cladding

Bolt-on or adhesive-fix cladding panels for retrofit hardening of existing masonry or concrete walls. Ceramic-composite and UHMWPE panel systems to EN 1063 BR4–BR7.

  • Panel weight: 18–55 kg/m²
  • Thickness: 20–55 mm
  • Protection: BR4 to BR7
  • Finishes: painted steel, stainless, anodised Al
  • Retrofit-compatible — no structural changes
  • Custom panel sizes to 2,400 × 1,200 mm

Secure Room / Safe Room

Turnkey construction of hardened safe rooms, panic rooms and secure communications rooms within existing buildings. Full 360° protection including ceiling and floor.

  • Wall: BR5–BR7 composite panel system
  • Blast door(s) with emergency egress
  • Vision panel / CCTV provision
  • Independent air supply and comms
  • Power and data connectivity
  • Sizes from 4 m² to 40 m²

Ballistic Partitions & Counters

Bullet-resistant transaction windows, teller counters and security partitions for banks, government service centres, embassies and police stations.

  • Glass ratings: BR2–BR5
  • Pass-through drawers and intercom
  • Aluminium or steel framing
  • Custom heights and widths
  • Anti-bandit (forced entry) options
  • Rapid site installation

Blast-Resistant Ceilings & Floors

Overhead blast protection using suspended composite ceiling systems and blast-rated raised floor panels. Used in critical infrastructure and command centres.

  • Ceiling blast load: up to 500 mbar
  • Fragment retention liner included
  • Integration with M&E services
  • Fire-rated options (BS 476)
  • Demountable for access
  • Non-conductive options for EMP zones

Perimeter Anti-Ram Barriers

Vehicle-rated hostile vehicle mitigation (HVM) solutions — surface-mount bollards, wedge barriers and shallow-foundation blockers for vehicle-borne IED threat mitigation.

  • Rated: PAS 68 / IWA 14-1 / ASTM F2656
  • Stopping: 7.5 T at 80 km/h
  • Fixed, removable and automatic variants
  • Foundation depths from 300 mm
  • Road-surface and landscape integration
  • Electro-hydraulic / pneumatic drive
Counter-UAS

Physical Anti-Drone
Suppression Systems

SMI's anti-drone systems take a physical suppression approach — hardened architectural structures that prevent drone penetration at the perimeter boundary without relying on electronic jamming or active kinetics. Suitable for nuclear facilities, military compounds and critical infrastructure where electronic countermeasures are restricted or unreliable.

Observation Towers

Blast-Resistant
Observation Towers

SMI's blast-resistant observation towers are purpose-engineered guard and watch towers for border posts, military forward operating bases, prison perimeters and critical infrastructure sites. The structure is designed to maintain occupant survivability under IED, mortar and artillery blast loads while providing 360° ballistic-protected observation.

Towers are supplied in prefabricated modular sections for rapid site assembly with minimal heavy plant. Custom heights from 6 m to 25 m with single or multi-storey observation cabins.
