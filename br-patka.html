Product Overview

Lightweight Tactical
Head Protection

SMI Bullet Resistant Patka is an innovative ballistic headgear system engineered for military, paramilitary, police, and internal security operations. Designed to provide enhanced mobility and operational comfort, the Patka delivers dependable protection while remaining lightweight and breathable during prolonged deployment.

The cylindrical protective design helps deflect ballistic impacts from multiple angles while reducing trauma risks. Protection is provided against AK-47 ammunition at the forehead and 9×19 mm ammunition around the skull circumference, with optional Kevlar enhancement available for high-threat environments.

The stable structural configuration supports optical aiming systems, surveillance devices, and tactical accessories while ensuring secure weapon handling in crouching, standing, and prone combat positions.
