End-to-End Security

Every Threat.
Every Terrain.
Every Requirement.

Since 1995, Secure Mobile India has offered a comprehensive portfolio spanning armoured vehicle manufacturing, bomb and blast protection systems, VIP security platforms, law enforcement vehicles, and custom defence fabrication — all designed and made in India.

Each product and service is backed by 30+ years of engineering expertise, DRDO/TBRL testing, MHA certification, and the trust of India's most critical institutions including the Indian Air Force, Navy, ISRO, and central police forces.

Core Services

What We Build & Deliver

01 · Armoured Vehicles

Bulletproof Vehicle Conversions

We armour a wide range of OEM platforms to your required protection level — from B4 handgun-rated through B6/B7 rifle-rated — without compromising ride quality, luxury, or original vehicle aesthetics. Every conversion is tested before delivery.

  • BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Toyota Fortuner, Toyota Land Cruiser
  • Mercedes G-Wagon, Jeep Compass, Mitsubishi Pajero Sports
  • Mahindra Scorpio, Tata Safari, and more on request
  • BR Lecture Stands (opaque & transparent layers)
  • Run-flat tyre systems, suspension & brake upgrades
  • Emergency escape hatches & blast undercarriage kits

02 · Bomb & Blast Protection

Bomb & Blast Mitigation

Our DRDO/TBRL-approved and MHA-certified bomb and blast protection range is used by the Indian Air Force, Navy, ISRO, and police forces. All products are invented, designed, and manufactured in India.

  • Bomb Suppression Blanket (BSB) + Blast Containment Ring (BCR)
  • Bomb Basket — IED & suspicious package containment
  • Blast Containment Bin — 2KG TNT rated, MHA 2013
  • Blast Resistant Tactical Bunker — IAF, Navy, ISRO, Police
  • BVCS — Bomb Velocity Containment System
  • Anti-IED and anti-drone blast mitigation solutions
View Blast Products

  • Bullet Proof Patka (turban protector)
