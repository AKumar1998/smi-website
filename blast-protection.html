With over 30 years of specialised defence engineering, Secure Mobile India has developed a comprehensive range of bomb suppression and blast containment products — all invented, designed, and manufactured in India.

Every product in our blast portfolio has been tested and approved by India's Terminal Ballistic Research Laboratory (TBRL) under DRDO, and tested to Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) standards. Our products are deployed across the Indian Air Force, Navy, AII, and police forces nationwide.

As the threat from IEDs, drone-delivered explosives, and improvised devices evolves, SMI continues to engineer solutions that protect India's people, infrastructure, and security personnel.

Product 01 · Bomb & IED

Bomb Suppression Blanket (BSB)

The BSB and Blast Containment Ring (BCR) minimise the impact of explosive detonation on surrounding personnel and property. The BCR is placed around the device and the BSB draped over it — directing blast upward while containing fragments and significantly reducing casualties.

  • Ballistically tested by TBRL — Approved by Govt. of India, Ministry of Defence
  • BSB sizes: 100×130 cm to 150×200 cm; BCR 43×100 cm to 43×150 cm (custom available)
  • Three protection levels; V50 of 415–660 m/s; total weight approx. 40 kg
Designed · Made in India

Product 02 · Blast Mitigation

Bomb Basket

The SMI Bomb Basket is a first-response tool for EOD teams — designed for rapid, safe containment and transport of suspected IEDs and suspicious packages. Its open lattice construction allows visual inspection without disturbing a device while containing secondary fragmentation.

  • Ballistic-grade steel and composite construction; operable by a single trained operator
  • Suitable for pipe bombs, hand grenades, and small IEDs; custom sizes available
  • Used by police EOD teams, central security forces, and airport security
Invented · Designed · Made in India

Blast Containment Bin

The SMI Blast Containment Bin looks like a standard public waste bin while providing certified blast containment — protecting high-footfall areas without visible tension or operational disruption. Tested to MHA 2013 standards and rated for 2 KG TNT equivalent.

  • MHA 2013 certified — rated for 2 KG TNT blast equivalent; DRDO approved
  • Superior all-around fragment containment; significantly reduces vertical pressure propagation
  • Low maintenance; designed for 24×7 public deployment in any high-traffic environment
