Product Overview

360° Cranial
Protection

The SMI PAS-GT Bullet Resistant Helmet is manufactured using specially woven aramid fabric laminations designed to provide advanced ballistic and fragmentation protection in hostile environments. Compared to traditional steel helmets, the lightweight construction significantly improves comfort during prolonged operational use.

The ballistic performance of the SMI PAS-GT Helmet provides reliable protection against fragments, 9mm pistols, revolvers, and SAF carbines up to a muzzle velocity of 425 m/sec. Despite its enhanced ballistic resistance, the helmet maintains a lightweight profile for improved mobility and reduced fatigue.

An optional SMI Visor (Face Shield) can be attached using a reinforced mounting system designed specifically for PASGT-style helmets. The visor swings up and out of the way when not required, offering protection against impacts, smoke, chemicals, splashes, particulate matter, and fragments.

PASGT PROFILE

The SMI PASGT Bullet Resistant Helmet is engineered to provide enhanced cranial protection for military, paramilitary, police, and tactical deployment environments.

Its extended side and rear coverage design offers superior fragmentation and ballistic resistance while maintaining lightweight operational comfort through advanced aramid fabric construction.

Developed for high-risk operational deployment, the PASGT profile delivers dependable protection against fragments and small firearm threats without compromising mobility, visibility, or long-duration wearability.
