Product Overview

Protection During
Public Address

SMI Bullet Resistant Lecture Stands are engineered to provide dependable ballistic protection for public speakers and dignitaries during political rallies, election campaigns, public meetings, and high-security events.

Designed with a premium wood-finish exterior and integrated bullet resistant glass, the podium maintains a professional appearance while offering enhanced protection against ballistic threats.

The lecture stand is equipped with a functional wooden lectern for microphone and lighting integration, making it suitable for both indoor and outdoor public address environments.
